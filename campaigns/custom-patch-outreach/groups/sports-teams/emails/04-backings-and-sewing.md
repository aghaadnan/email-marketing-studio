Patches on team gear need to attach the right way for how you use them. How it attaches depends on how you plan to use it.

**Hook-and-loop backing.** The patch fastens with a hook side and a loop side, and presses on and off. It is easy to take off and swap. This suits team uniforms where patches change from season to season.

**Iron-on backing.** Heat sets the patch onto fabric. It is a simple option for many caps, bags and jackets.

**Sewn on by us.** Prefer to skip the work? We can sew patches onto jackets, hoodies, caps and team uniforms for you, so your gear arrives ready to wear.

Not sure which fits? Tell us what the patch will go on and we will suggest one.
