A patch is only useful if it stays where you put it. How it attaches depends on how you plan to use it.

**Hook-and-loop backing.** The patch fastens with a hook side and a loop side, and presses on and off. It is easy to take off and swap. This suits uniforms and gear where patches change.

**Iron-on backing.** Heat sets the patch onto fabric. It is a quick option for many caps, bags and jackets.

**Sewn on by us.** Prefer to skip the work? We can sew patches onto jackets, hoodies, caps and team uniforms for you, so your gear arrives ready to wear.

Not sure which fits? Tell us what the patch will go on and we will suggest one.
