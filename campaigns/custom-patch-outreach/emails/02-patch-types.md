Picking a patch type is easier when you know what each one looks like. Here are the four we make.

**Chenille.** A soft, raised, fuzzy patch, like the letter on a varsity jacket. Great for a bold, classic look.

**Woven label.** Fine threads woven into a flat, detailed patch. A good fit for small text and clean logos.

**PVC.** A soft rubber patch with a raised, molded design. It is a good fit for bold shapes and raised detail.

**Dye-sublimation.** The artwork is printed into the fabric. It works well for full-color designs with lots of detail.

Not sure which one suits your artwork? That is a normal question. Send us the design and we will point you to a good match.
