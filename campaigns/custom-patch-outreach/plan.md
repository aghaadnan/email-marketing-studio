# Campaign plan: Custom Patch Outreach

Client: Epatchy (epatchy.com), Houston, TX
Channel: email only
Prepared: 2026-11-15
Status: draft for client review

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Name:** Custom Patch Outreach: "Bold. Crafted."
- **Summary:** A four-week email series that introduces Epatchy's custom patches to US business buyers and invites them to request a quote.
- **Primary goal:** 40 quote requests from business buyers by Sunday 2027-01-03 (four weeks from the first send on Monday 2026-12-07).
- **Secondary goals:**
  - Build a list of buyers who read and reply to our emails.
  - Learn which of the three audience groups responds most, so the next campaign can focus.
- **Note on the target:** 40 is a planning target. The client should confirm it once the real list size is known.

## 2. Target audience

Three groups of US business buyers:

| Group | Who they are | What they care about |
|---|---|---|
| Sports teams and clubs | Coaches, club managers, league organizers | Team identity, matching gear, patches on uniforms, jackets and caps |
| First responders and public-safety groups | Fire, EMS, police and security departments, volunteer groups | Clear, durable department patches, a respectful look, easy reordering |
| Brands and local businesses | Owners, marketing leads, merch buyers | A branded look, patches on apparel and caps, working with a reliable maker |

- **Buying stage:** mostly awareness and consideration. The one action we ask for is a quote request.
- **Where they spend time:** their work inbox, local and trade communities, and social media. This campaign uses email only.

## 3. Key messages

- **Core message:** Your team, your brand, your crew: custom patches, made with care. Request a quote.
- **Supporting messages:**
  1. **Many patch types, one place.** Chenille, woven label, PVC and dye-sublimation patches, plus embroidered keychains and blank caps.
  2. **Patches on the things you wear.** We can put patches on jackets, hoodies, caps and team uniforms, and sew them on for you.
  3. **Backings that suit the job.** Hook-and-loop or iron-on.
  4. **Your artwork, made stitch-ready.** Digitizing (turning your artwork into a stitch-ready file) is part of how we work.
  5. **A maker you can rely on.** Epatchy has been in business for over 8 years.
- **Proof points:** the product range above, the "over 8 years" claim (the client's own claim, used only in that form), and the client's own photos once supplied.
- **Held back until the client confirms them:** pricing, order sizes and delivery times. The call to action is always "request a quote."

## 4. Channel strategy

| Channel | Why | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| Email | Direct to the buyer, low cost, easy to measure, and it fits a small budget | Low to medium |

- No paid ads and no other channels in this plan.
- **Format:** short plain-text style emails with one link to the quote request page.
- **Sending:** practice mode only for now. No email goes to real people.

## 7. Success metrics

- **Primary KPI:** 40 quote requests by 2027-01-03.
- **Secondary KPIs:**
  - Open rate by email and by group.
  - Click rate to the quote request page.
  - Replies received.
  - Unsubscribe rate.
  - Spam complaints.
- **Tracking:** quote requests are counted from the quote page or inbox. Email results come from the sending tool's report.
- **Targets for the secondary KPIs:** set after the practice send, using the client's real list and past results.
- **Reporting:** a short summary at the end of each week, and a full review in the week of 2027-01-04.

## 8. Budget

- Small. Email only, no paid ads.
- The main cost is Claude and staff time for writing, review and sending.
- No amounts are set here. The client should confirm the budget before the real send.

## 9. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| The holiday season lowers attention and replies | Send mid-week, skip 12-24, 12-25 and 01-01, keep emails short |
| The list is smaller than needed for 40 quote requests | Confirm list size early. Adjust the goal or extend the dates. |
| Emails land in spam | Plain wording, no spam-style phrases, working unsubscribe link, real address in the footer |
| Claims we are not allowed to make slip in | Check every draft against `brand/voice.md` before it goes out |
