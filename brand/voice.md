# Epatchy brand voice

Write in plain English, to US business buyers: sports teams and clubs, first responders and public-safety groups, brands and local businesses.

## Our voice in three words

**Reliable, friendly, bold.** (Tagline: "Bold. Crafted.")

- Reliable: say what we do and do it. Short, clear sentences. No tricks.
- Friendly: talk like a helpful person, not a brochure.
- Bold: be confident about the craft, never loud about it.

## Words we use

- custom patches
- custom embroidered patches
- digitizing (turning your artwork into a stitch-ready file)
- request a quote
- your team, your brand, your crew
- Patch types by name: chenille, woven label, PVC, dye-sublimation
- Backings by name: hook-and-loop, iron-on

## Words we avoid

- cheap, bargain, discount-bin language
- hype and superlatives: "best in the world", "amazing", "incredible", "revolutionary"
- pressure language: "act now", "last chance", "limited time", "don't miss out", "hurry"
- jargon buyers won't know. If we must use a trade term, explain it in a few words.
- spam-filter trigger words: "free", "guarantee", "winner", "urgent", "no obligation", "risk-free", "click here", "buy now", "100%", "$$$"
- ALL CAPS words, extra exclamation marks, and "!!!"

## Claims we must never make

Until the client confirms them, do not state:

- prices or "starting at" amounts
- minimum order quantities
- turnaround or delivery times

Say "request a quote" instead.

Also remember: "over 8 years" in business is the client's own claim. Use it only as "over 8 years", never as a bigger number.
