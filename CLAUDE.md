# Epatchy Email Studio

This studio plans and writes B2B email campaigns for **Epatchy** (epatchy.com), a Houston, TX custom patch and apparel company. The goal is outreach to US business buyers.

## About the client
- Products: custom chenille, woven label, PVC and dye-sublimation patches, embroidered keychains, blank caps, and patches on apparel (jackets, hoodies, caps, team uniforms). Hook-and-loop and iron-on backings. They can sew patches on for you.
- Serves: sports teams and clubs, first responders and public-safety groups, brands and local businesses.
- Tagline: "Bold. Crafted." In business "over 8 years" (the client's own claim).
- Orders start from a quote request. Do NOT state minimum order quantities, prices or turnaround times until the client confirms them. Say "request a quote" instead.
- Physical address for footers: 10685-B Hazelhurst Dr. #42429, Houston, TX 77043. Every email also needs a working unsubscribe link (CAN-SPAM).

## Folders
- `brand/` - brand voice guide
- `leads/` - practice lead lists (made-up people only)
- `campaigns/` - one folder per campaign: plan, emails, per-group versions
- `notes/` - research and working notes

## Rules
- Write in plain English.
- Never send email to real people.
- Ask me before deleting anything.
